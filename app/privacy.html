Taylor PTO app: privacy

Charles G. Taylor Elementary School PTO, Foxborough, MA · Last updated November 18, 2026

The Taylor PTO app shows the school and PTO calendar, ways to give, and a private chat for Taylor families. It's run by volunteers on the Taylor PTO board. This page explains what it stores and why.

Calendar, Give and Connect

These parts of the app don't ask who you are and don't collect personal information. The app saves a copy of the calendar on your device so it works offline.

Chat: what we store

  • Your email address, used to sign you in (by emailed code or with Google). Only PTO board members can see it, so they can confirm you're a Taylor family.
  • The name you choose and your kids' grades, shown to other approved families.
  • Messages you post, visible to approved members of that room. The Board room is visible only to board members.
  • Notification settings for your device, if you turn notifications on.

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name and email address. We don't get access to your Gmail, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information, and there are no ads or trackers.
  • We don't use your information for anything except running the app for Taylor families.

Where it's kept

Chat data is stored with Supabase, our database provider, in the United States. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Notifications are delivered through your phone's or browser's own push service.

Deleting your information

You can delete any message you posted from inside the chat. To delete your account and everything linked to it, email board@taylorpto.org and we'll remove it.

Kids

The chat is for parents and guardians. Please don't share children's full names, photos or personal details in public rooms.

Questions

Email the PTO board at board@taylorpto.org.
