Holiday Shoppe

Kids shop on their own for small gifts for family, friends, teachers, even pets!

December 9 & 10·During the school day·Make-up shopping Friday, Dec. 11

For families

How shopping works

  • Prices $0.25 to $15, with most items $3 to $8
  • Payment Cash only. An envelope and gift list come home with each student. Send them back with cash by Tuesday, December 8.
  • Bring A shopping bag. Gifts come home wrapped and tagged.

Scholarships are available. Contact Mr. G. at mrg@taylorpto.org.
